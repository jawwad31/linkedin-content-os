# Operating guidelines — 2026-10-06

AGENTS.md owns architecture and instruction precedence; security-access.md owns access and data safety; design.md owns UI; product-spec.md owns approved behavior; feature-checklist.md and the current handoff own progress. This document owns content-quality and decision-record conventions.

## Content standard

Write for the selected reader, who may be a buyer, practitioner, operator, collaborator, or peer. Be specific, useful, evidence-based, and consistent with the user's approved perspective. Commercial relevance is useful when appropriate; it is not mandatory for every personal-brand post.

Never invent results, personal experiences, customer claims, metrics, quotations, references, beliefs, or capabilities. Distinguish external evidence from personal experience. A news source cannot establish that the user implemented a solution. A lead magnet is not automatically personal proof or a commercial post.

One post has one primary takeaway and funnel stage. Post type and source category are separate. A case study can serve Authority; a useful resource can serve Depth. Source mix is calendar configuration, not a permanent content rule.

Writing examples teach pacing, structure, specificity, and tone. Do not transfer another author's opinions or credentials to the user. Start with a small curated sample analyzed externally, then save an approved voice guide and a few examples. A scraper integration is not needed for launch.

## Shared generation process

Standalone and calendar creation use the same context resolver, structured brief, writer, deterministic checks, independent QA, and review process. An optional Ideas UI must not become a second implementation of generation. A simple standalone action may create the internal idea/brief automatically before drafting.

QA checks source fidelity, unsupported claims, voice, confidential details, duplication, hook delivery, and CTA/resource validity. Preserve existing approved resource-gating constraints; do not promise automatic delivery without an implemented service. Missing evidence produces a focused question, omission, or blocked claim, never fabrication.

## Research process

Follow the saved research prompt and explicit options. Prioritize primary sources, preserve links and dates, distinguish confirmed updates from interpretation, group duplicate reporting, and report coverage failures honestly. Suggestions in a briefing are not operational Ideas or draft posts. Human selection starts those workflows.

## Decisions and evidence

Use these states precisely: confirmed requirement; observed implementation; proposed technical choice; planned work; implemented awaiting validation; deployed awaiting manual checks; complete; deferred. An unchecked historical checklist does not establish that a feature is absent; a checked item does not prove today's deployment is healthy.

Record product decisions once in `docs/planning/product-spec.md`. Record technical choices and exceptions in the assigned phase/handoff. Historical owner/editor/viewer matrices are retired. Use the live capability model in security-access.md.
