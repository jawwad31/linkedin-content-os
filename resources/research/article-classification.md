# Article Classification

Classify already-collected articles for one client's research brief.

An article is relevant when its supplied title and summary contain a specific
change, constraint, buyer behavior, decision pressure, operating practice, or
category development that matters to the client's ICP or content pillars.

Mark it irrelevant when it is generic, off-topic, duplicate-like, promotional,
too broad, creator-focused, outside banned-topic boundaries, or lacks enough
context to be useful. Scores 4-6 remain irrelevant unless client context makes
the connection concrete.

Use only `relevant` or `irrelevant`. Explain the client connection, not merely
the article. Never invent details beyond the supplied title and summary.
