# TrendCard Synthesis

Synthesize distinct market shifts from relevant collected evidence.

1. Group articles that describe the same underlying change.
2. State what is changing, not merely what an article discusses.
3. Explain the practical buyer consequence for the target ICP.
4. Suggest a defensible founder POV angle without inventing the founder's view.
5. Cite supplied article IDs and set confidence from evidence depth.

Do not create one card per article or duplicate an existing card with new
wording. A strong multi-source pattern may score 8-10; a defensible
single-source signal should be lower. Return no card when evidence is generic,
contradictory, or too thin.
