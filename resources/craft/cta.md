# LinkedIn CTA and Closing Lines

<!-- playbook-section:universal -->
A CTA is the final reader step, not an engagement quota. Use zero or one CTA.
It must follow naturally from the argument and match the planned CTA type.

Universal rules:

- do not stack a question, DM request, follow request, and link;
- do not add a generic P.S.;
- do not ask "What do you think?";
- do not use follow prompts, continuation teases, or emoji bait;
- prefer a declarative close when an ask would weaken the post;
- comment gating is allowed only for a selected active approved Content
  Resource and only when the plan uses `resource_post`.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:selection -->
Select one CTA type:

- `diagnostic_close`: direct the reader to the first layer to inspect;
- `pov_close`: restate the desired belief as conviction;
- `specific_question`: invite context about the exact situation;
- `utility_close`: say when the framework or checklist will be useful;
- `open_door`: offer a genuine conversation;
- `comment_gate`: offer one approved resource for one keyword;
- `none`: end on the final insight.

Use `none` when no next step is useful. Use `comment_gate` only when a valid
resource ID is selected and recent-post gating allows it.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:diagnostic_close -->
## Diagnostic Close

Point to the first practical layer the reader should inspect.

Shapes:

- `If [specific symptom], check [specific layer] before [common response].`
- `Start with [diagnostic question]. The answer will tell you whether [decision].`

Do not claim the diagnosis is certain when the evidence supports only a useful
starting point.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:pov_close -->
## POV Close

End with the desired belief as a firm, concise final line.

Shapes:

- `[Old priority] is not the goal. [New priority] is.`
- `[Concise decision rule].`
- `Fix [root layer] before optimizing [surface layer].`

No question is required. Avoid repeating the hook word for word.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:specific_question -->
## Specific Question

Ask about the exact decision, failure point, or situation discussed.

Shapes:

- `When you made [specific transition], what broke first?`
- `Which of these criteria changed your last [specific decision]?`
- `Where does this handoff fail in your process?`

The question should make a useful answer possible. Never ask readers to merely
agree, applaud, or validate the founder.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:utility_close -->
## Utility Close

Tell the reader when to use or revisit the post's method.

Shapes:

- `Save this for the next time you are [specific decision].`
- `Use these checks before [specific event].`
- `This is most useful when [specific situation].`

Use "save" only when the body contains real reusable utility. Do not combine it
with another ask.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:open_door -->
## Open Door

Offer a real conversation without urgency.

Shapes:

- `If you are working through this exact transition, my DMs are open.`
- `Happy to compare notes if [specific situation] is where you are stuck.`

Use only when the founder genuinely wants the conversation and the post's topic
connects to the saved offer. Do not imply limited slots or guaranteed help.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:comment_gate -->
## Comment Gate

Offer one active approved Content Resource in exchange for one clear keyword.
Delivery is manual; never claim a bot will send it automatically.

Required elements:

1. Name the resource accurately.
2. State who it is for or what problem it solves.
3. Use the resource's approved keyword exactly once.
4. State that the founder/team will send or share it.
5. Make only one ask.

Shape:

`If you want the [approved resource title] for [specific use case], comment
"[APPROVED KEYWORD]" and I will send it over.`

Rules:

- do not use "free" unless approved;
- do not add "follow me" or "DM me too";
- do not hide all useful information behind the gate;
- do not promise immediate or automatic delivery;
- do not use this CTA without a matching selected resource.
<!-- /playbook-section -->

<!-- playbook-section:none -->
## No CTA

End when the final insight or conviction completes the argument.

Strong no-CTA closes:

- a concise operating rule;
- the desired buyer belief;
- a practical consequence;
- a carefully bounded prediction.

Do not append a question or ask after selecting `none`.
<!-- /playbook-section -->
