# Storytelling

Use simple structures that help the buyer understand a problem, feel seen, and leave with a sharper belief.

## Core Structures

### PAS

Use for buyer pain:

```text
Problem: name the buyer's specific pain.
Agitate: show the operational or commercial cost.
Solution: reframe the problem or offer a practical way to think about it.
```

### BAB

Use for transformation:

```text
Before: the old state or assumption.
After: the improved state or new belief.
Bridge: the operating insight that creates the shift.
```

### Contrarian Take

Use when the founder has a clear POV:

```text
Common belief.
Why it is incomplete.
What the buyer should believe instead.
Practical implication.
```

### Mistake To Lesson

Use only when the founder supplied a real experience:

```text
What happened.
What it cost or revealed.
What changed.
What the buyer can learn.
```

## Writing Rules

- One post equals one idea.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use plain language.
- Prefer operator detail over abstract advice.
- End with the buyer, not the founder.
- Make the commercial point clear without hard-selling.
