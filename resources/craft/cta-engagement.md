# CTA And Engagement

CTA and engagement tactics are useful only when they support ICP relevance and the business goal.

## CTA Rules

- Use one CTA at most.
- Match the CTA to the post's intent.
- Keep the ask natural and low-friction.
- Prefer buyer-relevant conversation over generic comment bait.
- Do not default to comment-gating.

## CTA Types

- Discussion CTA: ask about a real buyer tension.
- Save CTA: use for frameworks, checklists, or practical operating advice.
- Reflection CTA: use for POV or belief-shift posts.
- Soft commercial CTA: use only when the post has earned it and the business context allows it.

## Engagement Principles

- Meaningful comments matter more than shallow reactions.
- Saves indicate practical value.
- Dwell time improves when the post has tension, specificity, and useful structure.
- The best engagement comes from the right audience recognizing a real problem.

## Avoid

- "Comment X and I will send it" as a default.
- Broad questions anyone can answer.
- CTAs that attract the wrong audience.
- Multiple CTAs in one post.
- Engagement tactics that conflict with proof-safety or ICP focus.
