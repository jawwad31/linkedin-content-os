# Business Context

Use business context as background in every workflow. It is not a standalone content-generation mode.

## Fields

- Business description: what the business does and the operating context.
- ICP: the specific buyer, role, company type, maturity, market, and situation.
- Offer: what the business sells and the outcome it helps create.
- Approved Evidence: metrics, stories, client results, examples, testimonials,
  and claims explicitly approved for publication.
- Banned topics: topics, claims, competitors, comparisons, or themes to avoid.
- Founder voice: tone, vocabulary, cadence, and level of directness.
- Voice inspiration posts: up to three pasted posts used only to calibrate
  style, never as factual sources.
- Phrases to use: preferred language and recurring concepts.
- Phrases to avoid: banned jargon, overused claims, or off-brand wording.
- Content Resources: separately managed guides, templates, checklists, reports,
  worksheets, or tools that may support a resource post.

## Use Rules

- Treat missing business context as a constraint, not permission to generalize.
- If a required field is missing, use the user-provided input and flag the gap in proof notes or planning notes.
- Planning receives business description, ICP, offer, Approved Evidence, and
  banned topics.
- Drafting and review additionally receive founder voice, inspiration posts,
  and preferred/avoided phrases.
- Inspiration posts influence style only. Never copy claims, stories, metrics,
  examples, or distinctive wording from them.
- Research keywords are a Research-only setting.
- Never invent business context.
