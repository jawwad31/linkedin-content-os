# Business Context

Use business context as background in every workflow. It is not a standalone content-generation mode.

## Fields

- ICP: the specific buyer, role, company type, maturity, market, and situation.
- Offer: what the business sells and the outcome it helps create.
- POV: the founder or company point of view about the market, problem, or category.
- Content pillars: approved topic areas to stay consistent around.
- Approved proof: metrics, stories, client results, examples, screenshots, testimonials, and claims the user has approved.
- Banned topics: topics, claims, competitors, comparisons, or themes to avoid.
- Founder voice: tone, vocabulary, cadence, and level of directness.
- Phrases to use: preferred language and recurring concepts.
- Phrases to avoid: banned jargon, overused claims, or off-brand wording.
- Reference material: source notes, customer language, internal docs, market research, or founder notes.
- Publishing preferences: post length, format, CTA style, cadence, and risk tolerance.

## Use Rules

- Treat missing business context as a constraint, not permission to generalize.
- If a required field is missing, use the user-provided input and flag the gap in proof notes or planning notes.
- Prioritize ICP, offer, POV, approved proof, and banned topics over generic LinkedIn advice.
- Never invent business context.
